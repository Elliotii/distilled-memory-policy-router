### 3.1 project_memory vs task_state (3 cases)

| Case | project_memory unit | task_state unit | Risk |

| v05_sample_0018 | u2: "optimize for routing metrics" | u1: "LoRA rank 8 plan" | High — principle vs plan distinction |

**Audit note:** The distinction between "durable project design principle" (project_memory) and "current implementation plan" (task_state) is subtle. In v05_sample_0018, the agent labeled "optimize for routing metrics, not just loss" as project_memory — a design philosophy that transcends the current task. "Use LoRA rank 8" is a specific implementation decision → task_state. This classification is debatable and needs human review.

## 6. Top Risk Cases (5)

These cases require the most urgent human review:

### Risk 1: v05_sample_0014
**Issue:** "Currently reports X but not Y" → task_state vs "should show gold vs predicted" → service_memory. The boundary between current implementation gap and durable design specification is the subtlest in the set.

### Risk 2: v05_sample_0018
**Issue:** Implementation plan (LoRA rank 8) → task_state vs design principle (routing metrics > loss) → project_memory. The agent asserts these are categorically different, but a reviewer may disagree.

### Risk 3: v05_sample_0015
**Issue:** "Does not implement MemoryOS" labeled as project_memory. Could also be seen as task_state (current scope of work) or repo_memory (project documentation). Project-level scope decisions are the most contentious target.

### Risk 4: v05_sample_0006
**Issue:** "Uses synthetic service scenarios only" → project_memory. Is this a project decision or a temporary task constraint? Similar boundary risk to 0015 but lower stakes.

### Risk 5: v05_sample_0009
**Issue:** Two adjacent units both labeled service_memory. Are they truly both durable service behaviors, or is one a temporary state? "Single-turn only" could be a current limitation rather than a design invariant.

## 7. Agent Self-Limitation Note

These 20 sample cases were generated by an LLM-based coding agent (Codex/pi) as a dry-run exercise. The agent:

- Followed the v0.4 case schema and target guideline
- Applied the 5-target taxonomy to construct diverse cases
- Made semantic labeling decisions based on the target definitions in `TARGET_GUIDELINE.md`
- **Did not have human domain expertise** in memory policy routing or coding-agent memory management
- May have misclassified target-boundary cases, especially `project_memory` vs `task_state` and `service_memory` vs `task_state`
- Used synthetic content (project names, service names, phone numbers, etc.) that may not reflect real coding-agent scenarios

**These cases are NOT final gold labels.** They are a pipeline validation exercise. Every target-boundary case marked "Human Review: Yes" in the per-case table must be reviewed by a human before being used in training.

## 8. Recommendations

1. **Human review of all 5 risk cases** before any training data generation
2. **Spot-check 5 random non-risk cases** to verify agent labeling quality
3. **Define clearer target-boundary heuristics** for project_memory vs task_state, based on reviewer feedback
4. **Create a small reviewer guide** with examples of correct vs incorrect boundary decisions
5. **Do not scale to 500+ cases** until reviewer confirms the 20-case approach is sound
