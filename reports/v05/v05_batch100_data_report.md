### Range Deviation Explanations

**service_memory (89, above range):** The batch naturally has many durable service behaviors. This is expected in a coding-agent domain where "add feature X" and "the service must Y" are the most common types of durable information. New50 cases label new service capabilities as service_memory rather than task_state, per TARGET_GUIDELINE rule: durable behavior changes → service_memory. To reduce service_memory in the next scale, consider adding more "current progress" and "blocker" type task_state cases.

**task_state (50, below range):** Task_state is under-represented because many "add X" and "implement Y" units were labeled as service_memory (durable new behavior) rather than task_state (current step). In the next scale, add more cases where units clearly describe current progress, next steps, blockers, or temporary constraints.

**repo_memory (33, slightly below range):** Close to the range. Adding 2-3 more repo_memory cases in the next scale would bring it to 35-36. The deficit is small and not concerning at this scale.

## 14. Known Limitations

- **100 cases is still not a training dataset.** Target is 500-1000 for actual training.
- **service_memory inflation:** New50 labels many "add feature" units as service_memory, inflating this target. Consider whether some should be task_state.
- **task_state deflation:** Fewer "current progress" units in new50.
- **No multi-turn context:** All cases are single-turn.
- **Agent-generated labels:** Semantic labels may contain biases.
- **Source = `v05_batch100_dry_run`:** Not final train data.

## 15. Why This Is Not Final train/dev/gold

- 100 cases is ~5-10x too small for training (target 500-1000)
- No dev/gold split — all cases in one file
- Not locked — labels may change after audit
- Not distribution-balanced for training objectives
- Agent-generated, not expert-annotated

## 16. Recommendation

**Proceed to semantic audit** (companion report) to identify high-risk cases, then make a scale decision on whether to proceed to 500+ training data generation. The batch100 is structurally sound and domain-diverse. The main concern is service_memory/task_state ratio, which should be addressed in the next scale step.
